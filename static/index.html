Pose-Not-Pixels

Gait / Timed Up and Go trend and knee & hip arthritis kinematics screening — from skeleton landmarks only. Video never leaves the device and never reaches a model.

⚠️ PROOF OF CONCEPT — NOT CLINICALLY TESTED

This tool has not been tested clinically and is a proof-of-concept for research and software demonstration only. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat, or rule out any condition, and its outputs may be wrong — including false positives and false negatives.

Do not use it to make decisions about your health. For any medical issue or concern, seek a licensed healthcare professional. In an emergency, go to your nearest emergency room or dial 911 immediately.

All screening thresholds in this product are app-defined and UNVALIDATED, except the STEADI TUG value, which is a screening flag only (see below). Accuracy figures for this product do not exist yet — no validated performance numbers are claimed. Results below are screening patterns for clinician review only.

Capture guide (read before recording)

  • Camera: phone on a stable surface or tripod, hip height, 3–4 metres from the walk path, framing the whole body. Front pass: walk toward the camera. Side pass: walk across the frame. TUG: chair, 3-metre walk, turn, walk back, sit.
  • Clothing: fitted clothes; loose trousers and long coats hide knees and hips and degrade the landmarks.
  • Walking aids: note any cane, walker or helper in the capture notes — aids change gait numbers and can occlude ankles. A capture with an aid is not comparable to one without.
  • 2D-pose proxy limits: a single phone camera gives a 2D proxy, not lab motion capture. Frontal alignment and range estimates degrade with camera angle, distance and occlusion. Repeat captures with the same phone, same setup, same shoes.
  • Privacy: in the real integration path, video is converted to landmarks on the device and deleted by default. This demo uses synthetic landmark JSON only — no video is uploaded anywhere.
